<div>
<p><span style = "float:left; width: 0.7em; font-size: 50px; line-height: 100%; margin:5px 5px 5px 5px;">E</span>lectron cryo-microscopy (cryoEM) is a promising technique to study the 3-dimensional structure of macromolecular complexes. It is complementary to the X-ray crystallography and Nuclear Magnetic Resonance techniques. My group is interested in developing the computational approaches to derive the protein structures from the volumetric data obtained by this technique. At the medium resolution such as 5-10&Aring;, it is extremely difficult to determine the structure directly from the volumetric data. We have developed computational methods and tools in different components of the task towards the automatic determination of protein structures from the cryoEM density maps.</p>
